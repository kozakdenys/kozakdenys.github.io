claude-emails

claude-emails is a personal, non-commercial tool built and used only by its owner, Denys Kozak. Its purpose is to help the owner keep track of his own work: it reads the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, with his consent, and turns requests and meetings into a private task list and a daily schedule summary.

How it works

  • The owner signs in with his own Google account and grants access via Google OAuth.
  • The tool searches and reads the owner's Gmail messages to find requests addressed to him.
  • The tool reads the owner's Google Calendar events to list upcoming meetings.
  • The resulting task notes are stored only on the owner's devices and in his private repository.

The tool is not offered to the public; no one else signs in to it. This page needs no login.
